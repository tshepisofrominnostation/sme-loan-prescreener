Indicative pre-screen only — human judgment is final. This tool does not approve or decline loans; it helps a qualified credit officer decide faster. Production deployment would require compliance with the National Credit Act (NCA) and POPIA (out of scope for this prototype).

MODEL TRANSPARENCY & FAIRNESS

  • Deployed model: logistic regression over four auditable ratios (instalment coverage, operating margin, debt burden, loan-to-revenue) plus trading years and prior history. Every factor maps 1:1 to a model input.
  • Held-out test (200 SMEs): ROC-AUC 0.72, precision 0.49, recall 0.64 — recall is weighted above precision so fundable SMEs are not wrongly discouraged (false negatives harm the applicant).
  • Fairness audit (see repo): false-negative rates are monitored by sector and business size; business-size proxies (raw Rand amounts) are excluded from the model — only scale-free ratios are used.
